# Google Dorking

Google Dorking is the use of advanced Google search operators to locate publicly available information.

Common operators include:

## site:

Search only within a specific website.

Example

site:openai.com

---

## inurl:

Search URLs containing a specific keyword.

Example

inurl:login

---

## intext:

Search page contents.

Example

intext:"cyber security"

---

## allintitle:

Search page titles.

Example

allintitle:ethical hacking

---

## filetype:

Search specific document types.

Example

filetype:pdf cybersecurity

---

## Why It Matters

Security professionals use these operators to understand what information is publicly accessible.

These techniques should always be used responsibly and ethically.
