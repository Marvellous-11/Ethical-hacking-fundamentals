# WHOIS

## What is WHOIS?

WHOIS is a protocol and service that provides publicly available registration information about a domain name.

It allows security professionals, administrators and researchers to discover information about a registered domain.

---

## Why is WHOIS Important?

WHOIS helps us understand:

- Domain ownership information (when publicly available)
- Domain registrar
- Registration date
- Expiration date
- Name Servers
- Domain Status

---

## Example

Suppose we search:

openai.com

A WHOIS lookup may contain:

- Registrar
- Creation Date
- Updated Date
- Expiry Date
- Name Servers

---

## Why Ethical Hackers Use WHOIS

- Identify domains
- Understand target infrastructure
- Discover name servers
- Verify ownership information
- Gather intelligence during Reconnaissance

---

## Ethical Considerations

WHOIS should only be used responsibly and ethically.

Information obtained should never be used for malicious purposes.
