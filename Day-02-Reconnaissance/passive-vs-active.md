# Passive vs Active Reconnaissance

## Passive Reconnaissance

Passive reconnaissance involves collecting information without directly interacting with the target.

Examples include:

- Google Search
- LinkedIn
- WHOIS
- Archive.org
- Company Website

Advantages

- Difficult to detect
- Safe
- Does not alert the target

---

## Active Reconnaissance

Active reconnaissance involves directly interacting with the target.

Examples include:

- Port Scanning
- DNS Enumeration
- Banner Grabbing
- Network Scanning

Advantages

- More detailed information
- Better understanding of live services

Disadvantages

- Can be detected
- May trigger alerts
